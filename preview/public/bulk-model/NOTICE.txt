Deck Sage derives the average of the center/context tables, normalizes each vector, and stores float16 data with a canonical Oracle-ID lookup. The original repository's custom Python is not used by the site.
Training corpus: described by the uploader as Commander deck co-occurrence; individual training lists are not identified. Presence in the table does not establish rules understanding or current gameplay quality.
