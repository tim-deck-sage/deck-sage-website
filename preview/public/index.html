FIND DECKS IN YOUR BULK

Discover Commander decks in the cards you already own.

Sign in and add cards to My Bulk, then find possible Commander deck cores using learned card relationships. Review owned-card coverage, legal command zones and missing cards before you choose a plan. Some proposals are incomplete; a filled list is not a promise of playability.

Find decks in My Bulk

Free to use, with AI where it helps

Scan card names on your device and try the optional AI Gameplay Coach on a compatible WebGPU browser. Card search, collection management and rules-based deck checks work alongside AI assistance; not every tool uses a language model.

Browse the public library without signing in. Sign in to manage your private decks and collection. Public guides never include your saved cards or account details.

Website tools are free to use. Physical cards cost money, and purchasing them is optional. Affiliate link — Deck Sage may earn a commission on qualifying purchases.
