# Bulk deck discovery: app-to-web checklist

| Capability | Mobile app reference | Website result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find owned standalone commanders | `bulkCommanderDiscovery` | Reused directly; scans owned commander candidates without changing inventory. |
| Build a balanced owned candidate pool | `bulkCandidatePool` (`phase2-v1`) | Reused directly, including its role buckets, evidence, deterministic ordering, and 50-card target. |
| Qualifying threshold | App stops at a read-only candidate pool | Website requires at least 50 genuinely owned legal slots; 49 fails and 50 passes. |
| Commander legality | App excludes unsupported paired configurations from standalone discovery | Website validates singleton/color identity, Commander legality metadata, Oracle copy exceptions, Partner, Partner With, Friends Forever, Background, and Doctor command zones. |
| Complete proposal | Not present in the app screen | Website produces exactly 100 slots including one or two command-zone cards, with a deterministic basic-land plan. |
| Unowned commander | Not present in owned-commander discovery | Library-picked commanders may be unowned and are labeled in the purchase/trade list. |
| Strategy and gaps | Candidate reasons and bucket diagnostics | Website presents an archetype direction, owned evidence, role gaps, and missing land coverage before confirmation. |
| Purchase/trade plan | Not present | Missing cards are listed; known snapshot prices are totaled and unavailable prices remain explicitly unknown. |
| Inventory movement | App pool is read-only | Preview and cancel are read-only. Confirm revalidates exact rows, moves only reviewed copies, and creates one deck in one shared-workspace revision. |
| Cross-device safety | Not part of the app screen | Stable operation receipts, optimistic revision checks, response-loss recovery, and username isolation are verified against Supabase TEST. No browser-only fallback is allowed for confirmation. |
| Responsive UI | Native mobile screen | Fantasy-library web presentation tested in Core's browser at 1440 x 1000 and 390 x 844 with no horizontal overflow. |

Screenshot evidence is generated by `tests/regression/bulk-deck-discovery-ui.cjs` as `bulk-deck-discovery-desktop.png` and `bulk-deck-discovery-mobile.png`.

Known honest limitation: price coverage comes from the bundled card-data snapshot. Missing prices are shown as unknown rather than zero, and the user must still review the proposal before acquiring cards.
